# Gudnayber - Site Instructions
# Love is a choice.

# Site Purpose
Purpose: Help readers recognize the dignity of other people and choose practical neighbor-love across suffering, difference, and division.

# Content Structure
Content-Type: Source-grounded reflections and real-world stories
Categories: Dignity, Division, Unity
Required-Source: Every article must use a credible primary source as its factual foundation
Format: Markdown articles connecting verified human experience, moral reflection, practical action, reflection questions, and a neighborly practice

# Editorial Pillars
Dignity: Love shown to people who are sick, poor, stigmatized, isolated, or ostracized; preserve agency and reject pity or savior language.
Division: Political, religious, racial, and economic differences; address disagreement and injustice honestly without stereotypes, contempt, partisan endorsement, or false equivalence.
Unity: Verified real-world examples of people from different backgrounds and circumstances working together to make their communities or the wider world better.

# Editorial Commitments
- Love is treated as a deliberate practice, not a vague sentiment.
- Writing is rooted in a welcoming Christian moral imagination while respecting readers of every faith and no faith.
- Faith must never be used to shame, coerce, exclude, or claim moral superiority.
- People are never reduced to a diagnosis, hardship, demographic, ideology, or label.
- Articles invite honest reflection and end with one achievable act of neighbor-love.
- Factual claims must be supported by the listed primary source.
